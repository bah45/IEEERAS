Design a modern, premium, responsive website for **IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (IEEE RAS)** using publicly available information about IEEE RAS and its student/community activities.

The website should feel like a **professional robotics and technology organization**, combining IEEE's credibility with a futuristic robotics aesthetic. The design must be clean, structured, highly visual, and suitable for students, researchers, engineers, and robotics enthusiasts.

## 1. DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a visual identity inspired by:

* Robotics
* Artificial intelligence
* Automation
* Engineering
* Research
* Innovation
* IEEE's professional identity

Style:

* Modern
* Futuristic but professional
* Minimal and clean
* Technical
* Premium university/technology organization aesthetic
* Strong typography
* Generous whitespace
* Subtle futuristic visual effects

Avoid:

* Excessive neon effects
* Overly complicated layouts
* Generic corporate templates
* Excessive gradients
* Cartoon-style robotics graphics
* Cluttered dashboards

Use a primarily **white/light interface** with strong IEEE-inspired red accents, dark navy/charcoal text, and subtle gray surfaces. Include a dark-mode concept where appropriate.

## 3. DESKTOP FRAME

Design the primary website at:

1440 × 1024 px

Use a 12-column responsive grid with:

* 80px outer margins
* Consistent spacing
* Maximum content width around 1280px

Also create responsive versions for:

* Tablet: 768px
* Mobile: 390px

Ensure the layout is genuinely responsive rather than simply scaled down.
